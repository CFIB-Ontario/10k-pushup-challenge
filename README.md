# 10K Pushup Challenge Tracker - Analytics Dashboard

A comprehensive, data-driven web app to track your progress toward completing 10,000 pushups with full analytics and visualizations.

## Features

### Core Tracking
- **Live Progress Tracking** - See your total pushups, remaining count, and completion percentage
- **Session History** - View all your pushup sessions with date and time stamps (latest 50 shown)
- **Quick Add Buttons** - Rapid entry with preset buttons (+10, +20, +25, +50, +100)
- **Custom Amounts** - Add any custom number of pushups
- **Progress Bar** - Visual representation of your journey to 10,000

### Analytics & Metrics
- **Streak Tracking** - Current consecutive days streak
- **Days Active** - Total number of days you've worked out
- **Average Per Day** - Your daily average pushup count
- **Best Session** - Your personal record for a single session
- **Completion Projection** - Estimated completion date based on your current pace

### Visualizations
- **Weekly Trend Chart** - Bar chart showing last 7 days of activity
- **Activity Calendar** - GitHub-style heatmap showing last 28 days (intensity based on volume)
- **Color-coded Days** - Darker colors indicate higher pushup volume

### Data Persistence
- **Automatic Saving** - All data stored locally in browser (localStorage)
- **Privacy First** - Nothing sent to servers, all data stays on your device
- **Milestone Celebrations** - Visual celebrations at every 1,000 pushups and when you hit 10,000!

### Mobile Friendly
- Responsive design works great on phones, tablets, and desktop
- Touch-friendly interface
- Optimized layouts for all screen sizes

## How to Use

1. **Open the App** - Simply open `index.html` in any web browser
2. **Log Your Pushups** - Use quick-add buttons or enter a custom amount
3. **Track Your Progress** - Watch your stats, charts, and calendar update in real-time
4. **Build Your Streak** - Work out daily to maintain and grow your streak
5. **Analyze Your Performance** - Review weekly trends and adjust your routine
6. **Keep Going** - Your data is automatically saved in your browser
7. **Reset** - Use the reset button if you want to start a new challenge

## What Gets Tracked

- **Every Session**: Date, time, and number of pushups
- **Daily Stats**: Total per day, sessions per day
- **Overall Progress**: Total pushups, completion percentage, days active
- **Streaks**: Current consecutive days, tracking consistency
- **Performance**: Best session, daily average, weekly trends
- **Projections**: Estimated completion date based on current pace

## Installation

No installation required! Just download and open `index.html` in your browser.

### Option 1: Direct Download
1. Download `index.html`
2. Double-click to open in your default browser
3. Bookmark it for easy access

### Option 2: GitHub Pages (Host it online)
1. Upload `index.html` to your GitHub repository
2. Go to Settings → Pages
3. Select your main branch as the source
4. Your tracker will be live at `https://yourusername.github.io/pushup-tracker`

## Technical Details

- Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Chart.js for data visualization
- Uses localStorage for data persistence
- Fully responsive design
- Works offline after first load
- No backend required
- No dependencies except Chart.js (loaded from CDN)

## Browser Support

Works in all modern browsers:
- Chrome
- Firefox
- Safari
- Edge

## Privacy

All data is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Your pushup count, history, and analytics are completely private!

1. **Log immediately** after each session while it's fresh
2. **Maintain your streak** - consistency beats intensity
3. **Review weekly trends** to spot patterns in your performance
4. **Use the projection** to stay motivated with your end goal
5. **Don't skip days** - even 10 pushups keeps your streak alive
